Product Overview

Silicon Dioxide (SiO₂) is one of the most widely used industrial mineral materials, available in multiple commercial forms including silica powder, precipitated silica, and amorphous silica. It serves as a critical raw material across construction, glass manufacturing, electronics, ceramics, rubber, tyres, paints, plastics, food processing, and specialty chemical applications.

Naturally derived silica powder offers high purity, thermal stability, and excellent mechanical reinforcement in mineral-based applications, while precipitated and amorphous silica grades provide controlled porosity, surface area, and dispersibility for performance-driven formulations.

Our consolidated silicon dioxide portfolio supports industries that require strength enhancement, abrasion resistance, flow control, anti-caking, UV stabilization, insulation, and formulation consistency with dependable quality and supply.

Key Features

  • High purity silicon dioxide content
  • Available in natural and synthetic amorphous grades
  • Excellent thermal stability and chemical inertness
  • Controlled particle size, porosity, and surface area
  • High hardness and abrasion resistance
  • Improves reinforcement in rubber and tyre compounds
  • Supports anti-caking and powder flow improvement
  • Enhances durability in ceramics, glass, and concrete
  • Useful in plastics for UV stabilization and anti-blocking
  • Suitable for broad industrial and specialty formulations

Forms & Variants

Silica

Finely ground silicon dioxide derived from high-purity quartz, used where whiteness, strength, thermal resistance, and mineral consistency are critical. Common in construction, glass, ceramics, electronics, paints, and cosmetics.

Precipitated Silica

A synthetic amorphous silica engineered with controlled particle size, porosity, and surface area. It is preferred for rubber reinforcement, tyre performance, plastics, food processing, and catalyst support applications.

Amorphous Silica

High-performance non-crystalline silica used for dispersibility, anti-caking, flow improvement, and formulation stability where a highly functional and adaptable silica structure is required.
