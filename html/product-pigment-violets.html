Safety, Handling & Storage

First Aid

  • Seek medical attention if discomfort persists.
  • Inhalation: Move affected person to fresh air and keep calm.
  • Skin: Wash with soap and water.
  • Eyes: Rinse with plenty of water while protecting unaffected eye.
  • Ingestion: Do not induce vomiting; seek medical advice immediately.

Fire Fighting

  • Suitable media: Water spray, Foam.
  • Unsuitable media: Full water jet, Carbon dioxide, Dry powder.
  • Hazardous combustion products: NOx, CO₂, CO, HCl.

Handling & Storage

  • Avoid dust formation; use proper grounding to prevent electrostatic discharge.
  • Store in dry containers away from ignition sources.
