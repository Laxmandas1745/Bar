# Pigment Yellows

## Product Overview

Pigment Yellows are high-performance organic colorants widely used in inks, plastics, paints, coatings, and industrial coloring applications. These pigments provide bright yellow shades, excellent tint strength, and strong dispersion characteristics.

Pigment Yellows are typically based on **azo, diarylide, isoindolinone, and benzimidazolone chemistry**, offering a wide range of yellow tones from greenish yellow to orange yellow.
