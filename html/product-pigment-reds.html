Product Overview

Pigment Reds are widely selected for their strong visual impact, clean undertones, and dependable performance in coatings, masterbatches, plastics, and printing inks.

Our Pigment Red range supports consistent shade reproduction, stable processing, and reliable dispersion for industrial applications requiring durable red coloration.

Key Features

  • Strong and attractive red shades
  • Good tinting strength
  • Reliable process stability
  • Consistent color development
  • Suitable for multiple resin systems
  • Good performance in industrial formulations
