# Caustic Soda (NaOH)

This document describes two forms of Caustic Soda:

- **Caustic Soda Lye** – aqueous solution  
- **Caustic Soda Flakes** – solid form

Both are commonly used strong alkalis in industrial chemical processes.

---

# 1. Caustic Soda Lye (NaOH)

**Physical Form:** Aqueous solution

## Overview

Caustic Soda Lye is a liquid aqueous solution of sodium hydroxide.  
It is widely used across multiple industries because of its strong alkaline properties and effectiveness in chemical processing.

## Applications

Major consuming industries include:

- Pulp & Paper
- Textiles
- Soaps and Detergents
- Petroleum Products
- Aluminum Metal Processing
- Chemical Processing
- Bleach Manufacturing

## Specifications

| Sr | Characteristic | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH) | 47.00 % min |
| 2 | Sodium Carbonate (Na₂CO₃) | 0.20 % max |
| 3 | Chloride (Cl) | 0.015 % max |
| 4 | Iron (Fe) | 10 ppm max |

## Packing

- Supplied in **tanker loads**

## Safety

⚠️ **Caution**

- Corrosive substance
- Harmful to animals and plant tissues
- Proper protective equipment should be used when handling

---

# 2. Caustic Soda Flakes (NaOH)

**Physical Form:** Solid

## Overview

Caustic Soda Flakes are solid sodium hydroxide pieces produced from concentrated sodium hydroxide solution. They appear as **free-flowing white deliquescent flakes** and dissolve readily in water.

## Features

- High purity sodium hydroxide
- Free-flowing white flakes
- Strong alkaline chemical

## Applications

Major consuming industries include:

- Pulp & Paper
- Textiles
- Soaps and Detergents
- Petroleum Products
- Aluminum Metal Processing
- Chemical Processing
- Bleach Manufacturing

## Specifications

| Sr | Characteristic | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH) (Dry Basis) | 99.50 % min |
| 2 | Sodium Carbonate (Na₂CO₃) | 0.40 % max |
| 3 | Chloride (Cl) | 0.03 % max |
| 4 | Iron (Fe) | 20 ppm max |

## Packing

- 25 kg or 50 kg HDPE bags
- Inner HM-HDPE liner

## Safety

⚠️ **Caution**

- Generates heat when dissolved in water (exothermic reaction)
- Corrosive to skin and tissues
- Use appropriate protective equipment when handling
