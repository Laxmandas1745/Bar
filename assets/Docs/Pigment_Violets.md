# First Aid Measures

**General Advice**

Seek medical attention if discomfort persists.

**Inhalation**

Move the affected person to fresh air and keep calm.

**Skin Contact**

Wash the affected area with soap and water.

**Eye Contact**

Rinse the eye with plenty of water while protecting the unaffected eye.

**Ingestion**

Do not induce vomiting. Seek medical advice immediately.
